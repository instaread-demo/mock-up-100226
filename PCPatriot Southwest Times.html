Sheetz opens in Dublin

News

Sponsored by

Eric Thatcher was today’s lucky winner of a $2,500.00 gift card from Sheetz in Dublin, during today’s grand opening ceremony at the company’s new truck stop facility off I-81 and Route 100. Thatcher, who had stopped by after a dental appointment said he just wanted to ask if Sheetz had EV chargers. Thatcher and his wife have five children and a large passenger van, so the $2,500.00 gift card is greatly appreciated. Thatcher was on his way to work at Fulfillment Roads Warehousing after his dental appointment when he saw that Sheetz was finally open. (Danielle Reid photos/Patriot Publishing)

Other drawing winners of $250.00 Sheetz gift cards are (below) Carli Dotson and 6-month old baby, Rori of Pulaski and Nikki Dicesari of Riner. Dicesari had been to a doctor’s appointment and stopped by Sheetz out of curiosity when she saw the crowd.
