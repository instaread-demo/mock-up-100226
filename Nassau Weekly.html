Letter From The Editor

Sasha Rotko
September 3, 2026

Dear friends,

 

Listen to audio version of this article

Wildfire haze has made this summer season one for spectating. Two hundred and fifty American years came and went, a milestone received passively, if willingly. In our first issue of the semester, I am happy to report that new contributors refreshingly shift our focus. 

 

Something about summertime makes me want to use lots of words that start with the letter s. It’s a sticky season, a sweet season, a time for serendipity and salaciousness and sports and the sand. 

 

Subject of this week’s Nass-induced fever dream: post-colonial cuisine, scrapbooking, and sexual fantasies of our lord and savior, Jesus Christ.

 

Sanctimoniously, 

Sasha Rotko, EIC
